## Stage 1 Visual Target

Stage 1 proves the character design plus a simple idle/walk read, not full animation polish. Use the supplied local state sheet and derived reference proxy to keep the four directions consistent. Split the coherent `8x4` final state sheet into 32 isolated character-only frames: 2 idle frames plus 6 simple walk-loop frames for down, up, left, and right. The two idle frames are `normal -> little 1-3px bob/squish`, returning to normal in the runtime loop. The bob/squish must be present in the grouped state sheet itself; do not create it after split by shifting, squashing, stretching, repainting, or otherwise changing frame pose/silhouette. Each direction must have a non-duplicated idle pair and at least 3 unique walk frames in the composed sheet. The validator rejects idle frame 1 if it squats too deeply by losing too much visible height, moving its alpha center too far, or dropping the cap/body too low.

Thalla must read at `64px` on mobile as an ancient gold-white mushroom-elf field-sprite leader with visible mushroom-elf biology, strong simple silhouette, grounded feet/base over the shared separate chibi shadow layer, dark warm outline, and restrained bone/gold/white/brown palette. Generate with a limited sprite palette: `12-18` artist-visible colors across the source sheet, fewer than `20` total design colors excluding transparency and chroma-key, with shared cap/robe/skin/gold ramps instead of many near-duplicate beige, cream, blush, glow, or gold tones. Reference sheets must keep compact source-sprite occupancy: prefer a `512x384` or smaller source sheet, keep each major visible character blob within the verifier's `128x128px` tolerance for the `96x96` sprite-box contract, and reject a `1536x1024` showcase canvas with four enlarged figures even if magenta coverage is high. Represent authority through cap silhouette, robe blocks, posture, and `1-2` flat mycelium/spore marks only; do not use royal regalia, crown jewels, forehead gems, brooches, chest medallions, pendants, jewelry-like cap crests, gold filigree, ornamental robe borders, decorative trim clusters, clasps, collar jewels, or repeated gold badges. Quality bar: in the composed field preview, the chibi must look at least as crisp, contrasted, and finished as the approved bushes, mushrooms, and gates. Avoid a blurry/downscaled sticker look; use chunky readable shapes, a thicker warm-brown outline, clear face/cap/robe value separation, and a stronger tiny silhouette. Use `docs/home-field-chibi-style-reference.md` for the desired squat field-sprite feel and BJD-inspired chibi doll simplicity, including the liked 2026-06-23 Thalla reference as a positive face/cap/robe direction and the 2026-06-26 previous-best state sheet as the positive compact grouped-sheet direction.
